Privacy Policy

ComboCrush: Combo Blast (package com.combocrush.game)
Effective date: October 27, 2026 · Applies to version 1.0.0 and later

This policy explains what information ComboCrush: Combo Blast ("the app", "we") handles. The short version: we do not collect, store, or sell any personal information ourselves. The only third-party service in the app is Google AdMob, which shows ads.

1. Information we do not collect

The app has no accounts, logins, or sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or files. We run no servers that receive your data.

2. Information stored only on your device

Game progress, settings (sound, haptics), unlocked specials, puzzle progress, and the local leaderboard (including any nickname you type for a high score) are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID
  • IP address, which can be used to estimate approximate (not precise) location
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)
  • Ad interaction data (ad views and taps) and crash or diagnostic information related to ads

Google processes this data under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • AdMob data disclosure

Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads on Android, or Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on iOS).

4. Device permissions

  • Internet: used only to load ads.
  • Vibrate: used for haptic feedback during gameplay.
  • Advertising ID: used by AdMob as described above.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data deletion

All game data is stored locally. To delete it, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. To delete advertising data held by Google, reset your Advertising ID or see the Google Privacy Policy linked above.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, please open an issue at github.com/gcerkez/combocrush-web/issues or use the developer email address listed on the app's Google Play page.
