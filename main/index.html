ComboCrush: Combo Blast

Version 1.0.0 • Official Launch
A GAME BY A JERRY — ENGINEERED FOR FUN

ComboCrush is a next-generation tactile block puzzle game engineered for intense combo flow and strategic depth. Clear horizontal and vertical lines, ignite cascading chain reactions, deploy tactical drawer weapons, and demolish dynamic obstacles across 4 distinct game modes.

🎮 Game Modes

  • Classic: Relaxing pure puzzle placement with zero obstacles or timers.
  • Endless Challenge: Escalating difficulty with dynamic frozen ice, stone, shield, and armored cluster obstacles.
  • Combo Rush: High-octane survival where losing your combo streak ends the round immediately!
  • Tactical Puzzles: Hand-crafted levels to demolish obstacles within strict move limits.

Blast Bomb

Adjacent Special Combinations (Fusions)

  • ⚡ Chain Lightning + 💣 Bomb: Fuses into a ⛈️ Thunder Storm!
  • ⚡ Chain Lightning + 💫 Sweep: Fuses into a 🌟 Supernova!
  • 💣 Bomb + 💣 Bomb: Fuses into a 💥 5×5 Heavy Blast!
  • 💫 Row + 🚀 Column: Fuses into a ⚔️ Cross Laser!
  • 🌟 Supernova + Any: Fuses into a 🌌 Gravitational Singularity!
  • ⚛️ Chain Reactor + Any: Cascading whole-board chain reaction!

Obstacles Guide

  • ⛰️ Stone Block: Immune to regular line clears. Destroyed by explosions!
  • 🛡️ Reinforced Block: 2 HP steel block. Damaged by line clears or blasts.
  • 🔮 Shielded Block: Energy shield resists normal clears; bursts from specials.
  • ❄️ Frozen Block: 1st clear thaws the ice; 2nd clear removes the block.
  • ⚔️ Armored Cluster: Heavy 3 HP block that awards a bonus Special upon destruction!
